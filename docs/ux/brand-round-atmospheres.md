# Brand exploration: Atmospheres

Status: **exploratory round; no new lock.**

Date: 6 September 2026.

This round sits on top of the existing gallery at `/ux/brand`. Gazetteer with
Soft paper corners and Nest completion remains the preserved leading lock; Col
remains the runner-up. Production `AppShell`, Geist, and product tokens are
unchanged.

The purpose of this round is to test complete worlds rather than swap isolated
colors or fonts. Folio worked as a concept because its type, page, rules,
completion, and emotional story all described the same object. Each atmosphere
below has to pass that same coherence test while moving much farther away from
Folio, Gazetteer, and Dawn Ridge.

## Coherence contract

Every direction carries one idea through:

- display and interface typography;
- background atmosphere and material;
- palette and contrast;
- geometry, corner language, borders, and elevation;
- week navigation and the first-viewport hierarchy;
- work-row construction;
- a completion mark native to the world;
- freedom, adventure, journey, or ascent without relying on marketing copy.

The seeded Thursday and work items stay constant, so differences come from the
system rather than different product content. `Tempo run` is interactive on
every page so the completion language can be compared directly.

## The nine worlds

### A1 — Glassline

Route: `/ux/brand/glassline`

Modern/minimal as a strict Swiss object. Inter, paper white, black ink,
baseline rules, square registration marks, and no accent color. Freedom is
unoccupied space. Completion snaps a smaller square into alignment.

### A2 — Harbor

Route: `/ux/brand/harbor`

Open water and departure. Fraunces italic meets Outfit; harbor navy, seafoam,
foam, and brass build the palette. The week is a set of tide posts, work is a
manifest, and completion raises a striped buoy.

### A3 — Ion

Route: `/ux/brand/ion`

Bright optimistic futurism rather than dark sci-fi. Space Grotesk, IBM Plex
Mono, blueprint grid, lab white, graphite, and one electric blue. Work is
telemetry, remaining items are T-minus status, and completion closes an orbital
ring.

### A4 — Neon Pass

Route: `/ux/brand/neon-pass`

Cyberpunk as a night-city route. Rajdhani, IBM Plex Mono, ultraviolet magenta,
electric cyan, scan lines, and chamfered terminal glass. It avoids Forge's
heritage serif and red slash. Completion ignites a diamond waypoint.

### A5 — Atelier

Route: `/ux/brand/atelier`

Old-school craft without returning to Folio's newspaper page. Cormorant
Garamond, Karla, plaster, raw ochre, oxidized copper, letterpress work orders,
and vermilion wax. Completion presses an atelier seal.

### A6 — Summit Night

Route: `/ux/brand/summit-night`

Mountain adventure after sunset. Sora and IBM Plex Mono sit in indigo air with
snow, ice cyan, and one warm hut light. Work forms a rope team against a ridge;
completion lights a lantern.

### A7 — Riverstone

Route: `/ux/brand/riverstone`

Nature as water over rock rather than a map or trail. Source Serif 4, Nunito
Sans, mist, wet slate, lichen, organic stone geometry, and shallow ripples.
Completion settles a pebble in the current.

### A8 — Helios

Route: `/ux/brand/helios`

Warm solar optimism. DM Serif Display, DM Sans, Mediterranean poster color,
long shadows, solar gold, and terracotta earth. It is warmer and more graphic
than Dawn Ridge. Completion opens a corona.

### A9 — Aero

Route: `/ux/brand/aero`

Freedom as altitude. Barlow Condensed, Barlow, brushed aluminum, flight navy,
cloud white, and aviation orange construct a physical instrument panel. The
week is a contrail and completion locks two chevrons on target.

## Comparison discipline

- Do not mix these nine into one hybrid.
- Do not treat a favorite font as a vote for the whole direction.
- Compare the complete phone object first, then inspect type, palette,
  navigation, rows, and completion separately.
- Keep Gazetteer and Col visible as the current benchmark, not as ingredients
  every new theme must inherit.
- A future selection can trigger an applied Spatial Plan kit. This round does
  not restyle production or duplicate every UX concept destination.
